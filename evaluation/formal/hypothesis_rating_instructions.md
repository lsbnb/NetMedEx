# Instructions: Hypothesis/2-Hop Blinded Rating Worksheet

## What this is

`hypothesis_ratings_worksheet.csv` contains the **real** answers already generated by the frozen
`formal-v1-gpt-5.6-terra` run for the 9 questions whose `question_type` is `hypothesis` or
`two_hop_path` (Q004, Q008, Q015, Q018, Q025, Q028, Q035, Q040, Q050) — the stratum specifically
designed to surface latent multi-hop reasoning, i.e. the part of the benchmark most likely to show
a NetMedEx-vs-Traditional-RAG difference that document-level retrieval metrics (Precision@5,
Recall@10, nDCG@10) cannot capture.

Each question appears **twice**, once per system (`netmedex_hybrid_rag`, `traditional_rag`), under
a blinded `hypothesis_id` (`Q0NN-A` / `Q0NN-B`). Which label maps to which system is recorded only
in `hypothesis_ratings_key.csv` — **do not show that file to the rater**, and do not let the rater
see `system_outputs.csv` or any other file that names the system. The A/B assignment was randomized
independently per question, so label order carries no signal.

## Task

For each row, read `question` + `answer_text` and score on a 1-5 scale:

- `novelty` — Does the answer surface a non-obvious connection, or just restate common knowledge?
- `plausibility` — Is the proposed mechanism/link biologically credible given what's stated?
- `testability` — Could a lab feasibly design an experiment to confirm or refute this?
- `research_value` — Would a working researcher find this worth following up?

Fill in `rater_id` and `notes` (a short justification citing something specific in the answer text,
not a restatement of the score). Leave a field blank only if the answer text gives no basis to
judge that dimension — do not guess to avoid a blank.

## Blinding

Judge only from `question` + `answer_text`. Do not try to guess which system produced an answer —
if you think you can tell, say so in `notes` but score on content, not on your guess. Do not open
`hypothesis_ratings_key.csv` before finishing all 18 rows.

## After rating

Merge the completed worksheet against `hypothesis_ratings_key.csv` on `hypothesis_id` to recover
`system`, then write out rows in the `hypothesis_ratings.csv` schema
(`system,question_id,hypothesis_id,rater_id,novelty,plausibility,testability,research_value,notes`)
expected by `evaluation/metrics.py`'s `score_mean_ratings`. Do not merge the key in before rating.
